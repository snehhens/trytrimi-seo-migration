## Key Learnings from Phase 2

### What's Working Well
1. **Quick Answer Format:** Users and AI both prefer immediate, direct answers
2. **Comparison Tables:** Highly shareable and frequently cited by AI
3. **Safety Content:** Builds trust and reduces purchase friction
4. **Academic Citations:** Strong E-A-T signals improve rankings

### Optimization Insights
1. **Cost Transparency:** Specific pricing in answers drives higher engagement
2. **Safety First:** Leading with safety/legitimacy addresses #1 user concern
3. **Process Clarity:** Step-by-step breakdowns reduce confusion
4. **Structured Format:** Tables and lists outperform long paragraphs for AI extraction

### Content Gaps Identified
1. Need more "how-to" guides with step-by-step schema
2. Opportunity for FAQ pages targeting specific medications
3. Could expand comparison content (side-by-side drug comparisons)
4. Video content with VideoSchema would enhance multimedia signals
